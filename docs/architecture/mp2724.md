# MP2724GRH functional reference

Source: repository `MP2724GRH.pdf`, MP2724 Rev. 1.0, April 29, 2024.
This guide covers firmware-visible operation; package drawings, layout guidance,
electrical tolerances and component selection remain in the datasheet.
The complete programming reference is [mp2724.h](../../Massage_pen_FW/User/Inc/mp2724.h);
[mp2724.c](../../Massage_pen_FW/User/Src/mp2724.c) supplies the register catalogue
and nonblocking register operations through the shared I2C engine.
Addresses, bit encodings, setpoint ranges, defaults and reset masks belong in the
header. This guide uses datasheet register/field names, with the `MP2724_` prefix
omitted. Board charging requirements remain in [charging.md](charging.md).

## Power path and startup

The IC combines a single-cell Li-ion/Li-polymer buck charger, NVDC power path,
battery switch (BATTFET), USB source detection and a battery-powered boost
converter. IN receives adapter power, SYS supplies the system, BATT connects the
battery, and BATTSNS remotely senses battery voltage. PMID is the power-stage
node; boost operation supplies IN through the reverse-blocking switch. VCC is
powered from the highest available IN, BATT or PMID supply and powers internal
logic. I2C becomes available when VCC passes its lockout threshold; power-on
loads register defaults. Factory OTP options can change those defaults.

With battery power alone, BATTFET connects BATT to SYS. With adapter power, the
buck converter prioritizes SYS. Below `SYS_MIN` (`CHG_CTRL1`), SYS is regulated
above the minimum and BATTFET operates linearly to charge the battery. Above
the minimum, BATTFET can turn fully on. When charging is disabled or terminated,
SYS tracks the higher of the battery voltage and the minimum, with a tracking
margin. `VSYS_STAT` indicates which side of the minimum the battery occupies;
it does not report a measured voltage.

Input startup qualifies IN, optionally runs the `HOLDOFF_TMR` delay
(`CHG_CTRL0`), detects the source, updates `IIN_LIM` (`IIN`), and asserts
`VIN_RDY` (`STATUS1`). Buck operation then starts if `EN_BUCK` (`CHG_CTRL4`)
allows it; charging additionally requires `EN_CHG`, BATTFET on and qualified
NTC inputs when protection is enabled. `VIN_GD` means a valid input is present;
`VIN_RDY` means source detection and the current-limit update have finished.

## Source detection and input limits

D+/D- detection supports BC1.2 SDP, CDP and DCP sources, plus non-standard
voltage-divider adapters. `AUTODPDM` (`VIN_DET`) selects automatic detection;
`FORCEDPDM` requests a new detection with input present and then clears itself.
`DPDM_STAT` (`STATUS0`) reports the classification. Detection can overwrite the
programmable input current limit.

The integrated CC controller supports USB-C **sink mode** and source current
advertisement detection; it is not a USB Power Delivery negotiation controller.
`CC_CFG` (`CHG_CTRL4`) selects sink operation; `FORCE_CC` (`VIN_DET`) must allow
automatic configuration instead of overriding the pins to high impedance.
Factory defaults disable CC operation and force high impedance. `STATUS4`
reports both CC advertisements. Default-USB advertisements use the D+/D-
result; higher advertisements select their corresponding current limits.
Without a useful detection result the IC uses the fallback limit described by
`IIN_LIM_DEFAULT`. `LEGACYCABLE` (`STATUS1`) identifies legacy cable attachment
from the timing relationship between CC contact and IN arrival. The host can
use D+/D- results to adjust the limit for non-compliant legacy cables.

`IIN_MODE` either follows the detected/programmed `IIN_LIM` or forces a fixed
limit regardless of detection. In follow mode, host overrides belong after
`VIN_RDY` and may need reapplying after detection, restart or CC advertisement
changes. A forced mode can exceed a connected source's capability; the board
policy must choose it deliberately.

`VIN_LIM` (`CHG_PARAMETER2`) limits how far the adapter voltage may sag.
`EN_VIN_TRK` (`CHG_CTRL0`) can raise that limit to track battery voltage. Reaching
either the input-current or input-voltage limit reduces battery charge current
before sacrificing SYS power. `IINDPM_STAT` and `VINDPM_STAT` (`STATUS0`) report
those regulation loops. If input power still cannot meet the system load, the
battery automatically supplements it through BATTFET. Adapter presence alone
therefore does not imply that the battery is charging.

## Charging cycle

| Phase | Behavior | Programming reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trickle | Recover a deeply discharged battery below the fixed trickle threshold | `ITRICKLE`, `CHG_PARAMETER2` |
| Pre-charge | Reduced current until the programmable fast-charge threshold | `IPRE`, `CHG_PARAMETER1`; `VPRE`, `CHG_PARAMETER0` |
| Fast charge | Constant-current charging; BATTFET may remain linear below the system minimum | `ICC`, `CHG_PARAMETER0` |
| Constant voltage | Hold the battery regulation target while current tapers | `VBATT`, `CHG_PARAMETER3` |
| Done | Termination detected; optional top-off can still charge | `CHG_STAT`, `STATUS2`; `TOPOFF_ACTIVE`, `STATUS5` |

`CHG_STAT` reports the phase. Actual current may be less than its programmed
setpoint due to input limits, die thermal regulation or JEITA reduction.
Termination requires `EN_TERM`, constant-voltage operation, current below
`ITERM` for the termination debounce, and no input-current, input-voltage or
thermal regulation. Completion updates charge status, releases STAT and pulses
INT. `TOPOFF_TMR` can continue charging after status already says done; check
`TOPOFF_ACTIVE` before treating done as zero charging current.

After termination, falling below the regulation target minus `VRECHG` for the
recharge debounce automatically restarts charging while input remains valid.
Reconnecting input or toggling `EN_CHG` also restarts a cycle. Top-off resets on
charging enable, recharge or register reset.

The safety timer is fixed for the phase below the pre-charge/fast-charge
threshold, and programmable through `CHG_TIMER` (`CHG_CTRL2`) above it.
Disabling `CHG_TIMER` disables both timers. Expiry stops charging and reports
`CHG_FAULT`. `EN_TMR2X` allows half-speed safety timing during input regulation,
thermal regulation or JEITA current reduction. Input removal, toggling
`EN_BUCK`/`EN_CHG`, or register reset resets the safety timer.

## Temperature qualification and protections

NTC1 is the primary thermistor input. `EN_PG_NTC2` (`CHG_CTRL0`) changes PG into
a second thermistor input. `NTC1_ACTION` and `NTC2_ACTION` (`CHG_CTRL5`) choose
status interrupts only or actual protection; factory defaults report changes
without applying temperature protection. The channels share `NTC_TH` thresholds
and `NTC_ACTION` warm/cool actions. `NTC1_FAULT` reports a temperature window,
not a numeric temperature. VRNTC powers the divider during buck/boost operation;
the datasheet does not establish live NTC monitoring during battery-only
operation with both converters off. Do not assume a readable NTC status is a
fresh temperature observation in that state.

Thresholds are voltage ratios to VRNTC. Datasheet temperature labels assume a
beta-3435 thermistor with a pull-up matching its resistance at 25°C; they are
not universal temperature measurements. The header names the voltage-ratio
encodings. With protection enabled, cold/hot conditions suspend charging and
boost operation. Warm/cool conditions can reduce charge current, regulation
voltage or both using `JEITA_ISET` and `JEITA_VSET`. `STATUS3` reports each
channel separately. When both channels act, the combined priority is hot,
then cold, then warm, then cool, then normal.

Other protections have distinct recovery behavior:

| Condition | Effect and recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Battery undervoltage without input | BATTFET opens and registers reset; normal battery-only operation requires battery voltage to recover above lockout |
| Battery discharge overcurrent / SYS short | BATTFET opens and latches; `BATTFET_DIS` is set; documented recovery uses shipping-mode wake events |
| Input overvoltage | Buck stops and `CHG_FAULT` reports it; normal input restarts the input sequence and clears this fault |
| Battery overvoltage | Charging stops and `CHG_FAULT` reports it; `BATT_OVP_EN` controls this protection |
| Die reaches `TREG` during charging | Current decreases, termination is blocked and `THERM_STAT` asserts; timer slowdown depends on `EN_TMR2X` |
| Die thermal shutdown | Converter and BATTFET turn off; operation resumes after the shutdown hysteresis |
| Charge safety timeout | Charging stops; restart/reset conditions are described above |
| Missing NTC / battery | `NTC_MISSING` reports a near-rail NTC input; `BATT_MISSING` detects repeated rapid terminations; neither is a precision measurement |

`LOCK_CHG` prevents increases to the charging parameters named in `CHG_CTRL0`.
Program limits before locking. Clearing the lock or a device reset removes it;
JEITA lock interpretation needs clarification as noted below. The register
catalogue is a reference, not an instruction to apply factory defaults as a
board configuration.

## Shipping, reset and watchdog

`BATTFET_DIS` (`CHG_CTRL3`) requests battery disconnection, immediately or after
the delay selected by `BATTFET_DLY`. This disconnects the battery power path to
SYS; an attached adapter can still supply SYS. It is not a guarantee that the
whole board is unpowered. Documented shipping-mode wake events are applying
input power or holding RST low for the shipping wake interval.

With no input and BATTFET enabled, holding RST low for the longer reset interval
briefly opens BATTFET and then reconnects it, power-cycling SYS.
`BATTFET_RST_EN` enables this feature. This SYS power reset is distinct from
`REG_RST`, which resets the IC's register settings and self-clears.

The watchdog initially reports expired at startup. Writing `WATCHDOG_RST`
(`CHG_CTRL2`) starts/restarts it. It generates a bark at three quarters of the
selected period and a fault on expiry, resetting some fields to defaults as
noted in the header's field comments. This can restore charging parameters and
disable boost or change detection settings without resetting every register.
Disabling `WATCHDOG` retains settings until another reset. A future application
must choose whether to service or disable this watchdog; this reference does
not choose that policy.

## Boost and connector impedance testing

`EN_BOOST` requests battery-powered output at IN when input is absent, battery
voltage is adequate, and enabled NTC protection permits it. `VBOOST` and `OLIM`
(`CHG_CTRL3`) select voltage and output current limit; buck and boost share
`SW_FREQ`. The converter first raises PMID, then soft-starts the blocking
switch to IN. Enabling boost does not implement USB-C source advertisement.

`BOOST_FAULT` (`STATUS2`) distinguishes overload/short, output overvoltage,
die overtemperature and battery-low cutoff. Overload retries before latching;
overvoltage recovers automatically when output returns to normal.
`BOOST_OTP_EN` enables latching shutdown at `TREG`; `BOOST_STP_EN` enables
latching shutdown at `BATT_LOW`. These two shutdowns leave BATTFET supplying
SYS. Clearing `EN_BOOST` clears boost faults; a restart must satisfy the startup
conditions again. `BATT_LOW_STAT` is a comparator status, not a voltage reading
or automatic battery disconnection command.

`VIN_IMPD` supports a connector leakage/impedance test, which can help detect
water. With buck and boost both inactive, `VIN_SRC_EN` sources `IVIN_SRC` into
IN. Reaching `VIN_TEST` latches `VIN_TEST_HIGH` and pulses INT. Clearing
`VIN_SRC_EN` stops the source and clears the latch. Input arrival or enabling
boost aborts the test and clears its state. The host must define settling time
and interpret the comparator against board leakage and capacitance; the IC
provides no calibrated resistance reading or automatic water-detection policy.

## Indicators, interrupts and I2C

`EN_STAT_IB` selects the STAT/IB pin function. In STAT mode it is open drain:
low during charging, released on done/top-off/disabled/boost operation, and
blinking for the suspended fault conditions described in the datasheet. In IB
mode it sources current proportional to the magnitude of battery current;
`BFET_STAT` supplies direction, and `IB_EN` controls whether it remains active
on battery-only power. An external resistor and MCU analog measurement are
required. The IC does not provide digital battery voltage/current ADC registers
or a state-of-charge estimate. Use the repository's battery-sense path for
battery voltage. PG mode is active low for a qualified input; NTC2 mode replaces
that indication.

INT is an open-drain active-low pulse, not a level that stays asserted until
serviced. Capture the event and read the relevant status bytes; no interrupt
cause FIFO or separate acknowledge register is documented. `INT_MASK` suppresses
selected pulses and does not make the corresponding status unreadable.
There is no documented general read-to-clear rule for these status bytes.

| Event | Status / transition | Mask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valid input changes | `VIN_GD` changes | None |
| D+/D- detection completes | `DPDM_STAT` result update | None |
| Input becomes ready | `VIN_RDY` asserts | None |
| Charge completes / recharges | `CHG_STAT` enters done / leaves done for charging | None |
| Thermal regulation begins | `THERM_STAT` asserts | `MASK_THERM` |
| Watchdog bark / expiry | `WATCHDOG_BARK` / `WATCHDOG_FAULT` asserts | None |
| Charge fault begins | `CHG_FAULT` leaves normal | None |
| Missing NTC / battery changes | `NTC_MISSING` / `BATT_MISSING` changes | None |
| Boost fault / OVP recovery | `BOOST_FAULT` enters a fault / OVP returns to normal | See inconsistency below |
| NTC window changes | `NTC1_FAULT` / `NTC2_FAULT` changes | None |
| Input regulation begins | `VINDPM_STAT` / `IINDPM_STAT` asserts | `MASK_DPM` |
| Top-off starts / ends | `TOPOFF_ACTIVE` changes | `MASK_TOPOFF` |
| CC advertisement changes | `CC1_SNK_STAT` / `CC2_SNK_STAT` changes | `MASK_CC_INT` |
| Battery crosses low threshold | `BATT_LOW_STAT` asserts | `MASK_BATT_LOW` |
| Impedance test reaches threshold | `VIN_TEST_HIGH` asserts | None |
| Debug accessory enters / exits | `DEBUGACC` changes | `MASK_DEBUG` |

I2C uses an unshifted slave address in the header, a byte register address and
byte data, with standard/fast-mode transfers. Reads write the register pointer,
issue a repeated start, then read data; the host NACKs the final byte. Multi-byte
transfers auto-increment the register address. Do not let a burst cross the
undocumented gap or write into status bytes. Preserve reserved bits, including
the nonzero reserved reset bit in `INT_MASK`. In read-modify-write, suppress
unrelated write-one action bits (`REG_RST`, `WATCHDOG_RST`, `FORCEDPDM`) so a
field update cannot accidentally reset settings, restart the watchdog or
repeat source detection. Field macros only pack/extract bits; callers must
validate ranges and avoid undocumented codes.

## Datasheet inconsistencies and limits

These uncertainties are present in Rev. 1.0 and must not be silently resolved
by inventing behavior:

- The shipping-delay register table labels the delayed setting as 10 seconds,
  while the timing table specifies 12 seconds typical. Do not use the selector
  as a precise shutdown deadline.
- The lock-function prose says writing `REG_RST` low unlocks parameters, while
  its register definition specifies a write-high reset action. Use explicit
  `LOCK_CHG` clearing to unlock; a register reset also restores its default.
- Lock prose says JEITA settings can only be reduced, but larger voltage
  reduction codes lower the actual target and current-fraction codes are also
  inverse. The reference does not infer the hardware comparison rule.
- `DPDM_STAT` and `CHG_STAT` rows say no interrupt, but the interrupt list and
  operation text describe detection-complete, done and recharge pulses.
- The boost battery-low prose calls its pulse maskable, while `BOOST_FAULT` and
  the interrupt list call boost faults unmaskable. `MASK_BATT_LOW` clearly masks
  the comparator event; it is not established to mask boost-fault reporting.
- Battery OVP prose and the electrical-characteristics table disagree on the
  nominal percentage. Protection tolerance is not encoded as a software setting.

The header includes every documented configuration/status command, including
reserved masks, valid codes and field-level watchdog reset notes. The
[hardware adapter](../../Massage_pen_FW/User/Src/firmware_hw.c) binds HAL
transport; [charging.c](../../Massage_pen_FW/User/Src/charging.c) implements
configuration audit/readback, watchdog service, status/interrupt handling and
shipping requests. Production configuration and hardware idle leases remain
unsupplied; remaining policy and hardware acceptance are recorded in
[charging.md](charging.md). Generated peripheral settings and pins remain in
the repository's generated files.
